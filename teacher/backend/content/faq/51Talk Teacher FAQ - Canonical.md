## FAQ-ACC-002 · What should I do if MyPage is locked because of attendance issues?
Domain: Account, Portal & Personal Information
Scenario: What should I do if MyPage is locked because of attendance issues?
Match phrases: What should I do if MyPage is locked because of attendance issues? | Attendance Quiz | Punctuality Talk | unlock MyPage account | SA Deliberation Coaching | attendance warning training | Reliability Self-Paced Video | MyPage locked due to absences
Source: Global Teacher Guide 101 FAQ.md
Approved answer:
- For a late-arrival or early-leave coaching case, book the required Punctuality Talk through “Book My Training.” MyPage is normally unlocked within 24 hours after completion.
- At the first warning, complete the Reliability Self-Paced Video and Attendance Quiz in Global 411. A passing quiz normally unlocks MyPage within 24 hours; otherwise the 3-day prohibition remains.
- At the second warning, complete the same video and quiz. A passing result normally unlocks MyPage within 24 hours; otherwise the 5-day prohibition remains.
- At the final warning, book and complete the Mentor-Reliability FGD-GAC through “Book My Training.” MyPage is normally unlocked within 24 hours after completion; otherwise the prohibition is 7 days.
- For an SA Review, complete SA Deliberation Coaching. Teacher Support sets the unlocking timeline.

## FAQ-FEE-005 · When will I receive my earnings? When is the payout?

Scenario: When will I receive my earnings? When is the payout?
Match phrases: When will I receive my earnings? When is the payout? | payout date | salary release | earnings cutoff | minimum 500 PHP | 10 business days | remittance schedule | when will I receive my earnings | When will I receive my earnings?
Source: DingTalk: 51talk-AI客服知识库（协作版本） | Global Teacher Guide 101 FAQ.md
Approved answer:
- Payout Schedule after submitting the bank info. Payout will depend on the cut-off when the HBT uploads his/her bank information.
- Example A: Bank details were uploaded on 12th of March. (Details uploaded ON or BEFORE the deadline). The HBT will be paid out for the current cutoff  (5-10 business days after the cutoff). Take note that the 5-10 business days do not count weekends and Holidays of the Philippines, China and Hongkong.
- Example B: Bank details were uploaded 14th of March. (Details uploaded AFTER the deadline). Since the deadline is 13th and 28th of the month, payment of HBT’s fees will be on the next cutoff, March 16-31.
- Deadlines for uploading the payment method are:
- 1st cutoff: on or before 12th  and  2nd cutoff: on or before 27th
- Processing of fees is usually done within 5-10 business days after the cut-off. Please note that a remittance fee of 1USD will apply since lesson fees are being remitted directly from Hongkong.
