# **Lesson Memo (LM):**

A **Lesson Memo** is a structured post-lesson evaluation report completed by a teacher (in this case, on the 51Talk platform) to document a student's performance during a specific class. It serves as a feedback mechanism that highlights what the student did well, identifies areas for improvement, provides specific corrections (like grammar and pronunciation), and assigns a general evaluation grade.

## **Guideline:**
- MyPage has a variety of ways to input lesson memos depending on the type of learning material. Therefore, **teachers** should be properly guided about this so they know how to navigate the page and understand how to provide feedback accordingly.
- Self-Access Module:
    - Lesson Memo 101: [https://edu.51talk.com/course/408](https://edu.51talk.com/course/408)
    - Lesson Memo 102: [https://edu.51talk.com/course/591](https://edu.51talk.com/course/591)



# **When Should a Lesson Memo be Accomplished?**

A lesson memo should be accomplished **immediately after the lesson concludes \[Read: after the 25th minute\]**.
The memo is designed to be a post-class summary submitted by the teacher to ensure the student (and their parents) receive timely feedback to review while the lesson is still fresh in their minds.

- **Successfully Ended Lessons:** Accumulated count of successfully finished lessons for the entire period.

Senior Teacher A: Same standards as Star Teacher D (Absence of 2.00% or lower, Late/Early of 0.90% or lower, Audit of 1 or lower, Complaints of 2 or lower).

Senior Teacher B & C: Stricter punctuality requirement (Late/Early of 0.75% or lower), while maintaining Absence at 2.00% or lower, Audit at 1 or lower, and Complaints at 2 or lower.

Senior Teacher D: Requires tighter attendance (Absence of 1.50% or lower, Late/Early of 0.75% or lower) and a stricter complaint limit (maximum of 1 complaint).
3. Super Star Teacher Level
Super Star Teacher A: Punctuality threshold tightened to 0.60% or lower for late/early rate, with Absence at 1.50% or lower, Audit at 1 or lower, and Complaints at 1 or lower.

Super Star Teacher B & C: Punctuality threshold tightened to 0.55% or lower for late/early rate, with Absence at 1.50% or lower, Audit at 1 or lower, and Complaints at 1 or lower.
4. Master Teacher & Leader Level
Master Teacher A, Master Teacher B, & Teacher of Teachers:

Highest conduct standards: Zero complaints allowed.

Punctuality rate must be maintained at 0.45% or lower.

Absence rate must be maintained at 1.50% or lower.

Lesson audit score must remain 1 or lower.
